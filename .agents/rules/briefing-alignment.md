# Rule: Briefing alignment

**Scope:** agent-requested — load when planning or starting a new feature, agent, or service.

1. Identify the department in `BRIEFING.md` the feature serves (Talent Selection, Training, Support, Sales, Marketing, HR, Technology, Executive).
2. Quote the "What they need" item it delivers; if none matches, stop and ask the developer.
3. Respect the AI constraints: CV rankings must be explainable; support automation is measured against the 24h SLA (48h today) and the 40% first-line resolution goal; training recommendations depend on client profile.
4. Note the feature in `memory-bank/progress.md`.
