# Nexova

## Why I chose Nexova
### I had two other options I was seriously contemplating: Brasaland and Trackflow. Brasaland because it is a B2C build-up, which I really like, and Trackflow because of its logistics background. I ended up choosing Nexova not because it was the one I felt the most comfortable with or liked the most, but because it’s the one where I saw the biggest opportunity to learn. In one sentence it's AI engineering heavy.

## Department problems I found most interesting
### I’m looking forward to building an easy-to-understand, user-friendly dashboard for HR to manage all holiday requests, absences, and HR queries. I’m also excited about the HR agent that answers all kinds of questions from the team.
### Resolving 40% of queries with 0 human interaction is also something I’m looking forward to doing for the customer service team. Here, I’m especially intrigued by the opportunity to use and learn about RAG!

## What automation and AI functions I’m looking forward to building
### Automating CV scoring with pipelines and creating rankings with the help of AI.

## My AI Agent Idea
### Set up a webhook where the CV file gets received --> this triggers the workflow. Then, I will scrape the text from the file and reformat it into TOON for token efficiency. The AI then looks for certain keywords in the TOON CV and only sends over the CVs for manual review that pass the criterias.
